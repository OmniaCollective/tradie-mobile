# Tradie v1.6.0 — release pack

Status (6 Oct 2026): rebuilt on the approved brand system, with Sign in with Apple, server-side voice, Suggest times and the new Free/Pro offer. Not yet built on EAS or submitted. Nothing ships until every box below is ticked.

---

## 0. What's in 1.6.0

**Offer:** Free = unlimited jobs, customers and quotes; 3 invoices a month; 3 voice jobs to try. Pro £9.99/month or £99.99/year = unlimited invoices, tax set-aside + VAT tracker, expenses and receipts with tax-year exports, unlimited voice jobs. No lifetime.

**App**
- Brand system v4 (`brand/BRAND-BRIEF.md`): light + dark mode, logo cyan, line icons. Every screen rebuilt: Home, Jobs, Money, Account, job details, add job, add expense, onboarding, paywall, Suggest times, all sheets.
- Tabs Home · Jobs · Money · Account; "+ New job" in the header; to-do list on Jobs.
- Sign in with Apple (onboarding with "Not now", Account, voice). Links RevenueCat with name + email; Delete account revokes Apple and deletes the RevenueCat customer.
- Voice jobs go through Tradie's server — the AI key no longer ships in the app.
- **Suggest times:** picks the 3 best free times from the diary, job length, working hours, iPhone calendar and Apple Maps drive times; sent by text; times pencilled in for 48 h; one tap to book with a confirmation text.
- Paywall: real prices only, Pro unlocks instantly, success only when Pro is active.

**Bugs fixed along the way:** onboarding never showed; calendar crash on SDK 57 (`expo-calendar/legacy`); iOS 17 calendar permission text missing; dates a day early in British Summer Time (21 places) and for US users; decimals couldn't be typed in rates; Clear All Data had no confirmation; sample data visible to customers; invisible booking screens removed; Groq retired the voice model; RevenueCat had no purchase key and products weren't attached to `pro`; dollar icon in a UK app.

## 4. App Privacy answers (App Store Connect)

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.
**Is any data used to track users?** No (no ads, no cross-app tracking, no data brokers).

| Data type | Collected | Linked to user | Tracking | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contact Info → Name | Yes (Sign in with Apple → RevenueCat) | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| Contact Info → Email Address | Yes (Sign in with Apple → RevenueCat) | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| Identifiers → User ID | Yes (Apple account ID, RevenueCat app user ID) | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| Purchases → Purchase History | Yes (RevenueCat) | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| User Content → Audio Data | Yes (voice jobs sent to Groq, not kept) | No | No | App Functionality |

Everything else: **Not collected.** Jobs, customers, invoices, expenses, photos and calendar stay on the phone, and postcodes for drive times are used in real time only and not kept, so they don't count as collected. Audio is listed to be safe even though it isn't kept.
