### Description

> Tradie is the job app for UK sole-trader trades. Send booking invites, quote instantly from your own prices, and turn finished jobs into professional invoices — all from your phone.
>
> QUOTES & BOOKINGS
> • Your own job types and prices, with urgent and emergency rates
> • Send booking invites by text or email
> • Let a customer book on your phone, there and then
> • Share professional PDF quotes
>
> JOBS & CALENDAR
> • Day and month view of every job
> • Sync jobs to your iPhone calendar
> • Add a job by voice — say the details, Tradie fills them in
> • Job photos, notes and parts
>
> INVOICES & MONEY
> • Invoices created automatically when a job is done
> • Send as PDF, track paid and unpaid
> • Log expenses and snap receipts
> • Export to CSV for your accountant
>
> TAX, SORTED
> • See your income tax and Class 4 NI as you go
> • CIS deductions and VAT (standard and flat rate) tracked
> • A monthly "set aside" figure so January isn't a shock
>
> Built for plumbers, electricians, gardeners, cleaners, carpenters, window cleaners, dog walkers and more.
>
> Tradie Pro removes the monthly limits on booking invites and customers.
>
> Tax figures are estimates to help you plan, not tax advice.
