Tradie is the job app for solo traders. Quote from your own prices, turn finished jobs into invoices, and know what to set aside for tax, all from your phone.

QUOTES AND INVOICES
• Itemised quotes and invoices: labour, materials, a call-out fee or a discount, each with its own quantity and price
• Your logo at the top of every quote and invoice
• Your own notes or terms, and how long quotes are valid
• VAT-registered? Set the VAT rate for each item, and use the reverse charge for CIS work
• Professional A4 PDFs, sent from your phone
• See what's waiting for payment and what's paid, with one-tap reminders

JOBS AND SCHEDULING
• Suggest times: Tradie checks your diary, your iPhone calendar and the drive between jobs, then texts the customer three times that work
• Times stay pencilled in until the customer replies; book with one tap
• Sync jobs to your iPhone calendar, with reminders
• Notes, photos and parts on every job, and the profit on each one

TAX, SORTED (PRO)
• Income Tax and Class 4 NI worked out as you go
• A monthly "set aside" figure so January isn't a shock
• VAT threshold tracker, CIS deductions (on labour only), standard and flat rate VAT
• Expenses with receipt photos, and a tax-year export for your accountant

SAVE TIME (PRO)
• A price list of the items you use most, added to any quote in a tap
• Add the parts you've logged to a quote with your markup

Built for plumbers, electricians, gardeners, cleaners, carpenters, window cleaners and more.

Free: unlimited jobs, customers and itemised quotes, your logo on every PDF, and 3 invoices a month. Tradie Pro adds unlimited invoices, the tax tracker, expenses and receipts, tax-year exports, and the price list with parts markup.

Tax figures are estimates to help you plan, not tax advice.
