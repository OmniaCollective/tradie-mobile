Tradie is a job, quote, invoice and tax set-aside app for self-employed tradespeople in the UK and US.

NO DEMO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Sign in with Apple is optional and is not asked for at the start. All jobs, customers, quotes, invoices and expenses are stored on the device only. Signing in (Account → Sign in) only adds keeping Pro on a new phone and real drive times in Suggest times.

QUICK TEST PATH
1. Welcome → Get started → choose a trade → enter any details → Start using Tradie (or Skip for now).
2. Home → New job → enter a customer and a job type → Save.
3. The job opens as a checklist: Quote, Booked, Job done, Invoice, Paid. Steps can be done in any order, and undone.
4. Send quote (PDF via the share sheet). Booked: Suggest times picks three free slots and drafts a text, or Pick a time myself.
5. New in 1.7: on the job, Price → Edit opens the price editor. Add item → New item (labour, materials or other, with quantity, unit and price), or Discount (amount or percentage). Arrange reorders items. Save, then Send quote to see the itemised PDF.
6. Mark done → Send invoice → Mark paid.
7. Money tab: what you're owed, what's been paid, and (with Pro) the tax set-aside, expenses and the tax-year export. Tap an invoice for Send, Chase payment, Mark paid and Delete. Sending or chasing asks for business and payment details once if they are missing.
8. Account → Quotes and invoices: add a logo (chosen from Photos), a note for quotes and for invoices, and how long quotes are valid. They appear on the PDFs.
9. Account → Insurance and licences: add one to see expiry reminders.

SUBSCRIPTION (Tradie Pro, auto-renewable, monthly or yearly)
Free: unlimited jobs, itemised quotes, customers and booking, a logo and notes on PDFs, and 3 invoices a month (an invoice counts the first time its PDF is sent or shared). Pro adds unlimited invoices, the tax set-aside (and VAT tracker in the UK), expenses with receipt photos, the tax-year export for an accountant, and (new in 1.7) a saved price list and adding logged parts to a price with a markup.
Open the paywall from Account → Plan → See Pro prices, tap Tax set-aside or Expenses on the Money tab, or tap "From your price list" in the price editor. The paywall shows the price, renewal terms, Restore purchase, Terms of use and Privacy policy. Sandbox purchases work as normal.
Tax-year export (Pro): Money → Export the tax year for your accountant → This or Last tax year. It shares one zip: a summary PDF, a spreadsheet of income and expenses, and receipt photos.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Account → Sign in → Delete account (shown when signed in). This revokes Sign in with Apple, deletes the server-side subscription record and erases all on-device data, and tells the user to cancel any subscription in Apple ID settings. Account → Delete all my data erases the on-device data without an account.

PERMISSIONS (each is asked only when the feature is used)
Calendar: add booked jobs and check busy times for Suggest times. Camera and photos: receipts, job photos and the business logo (the logo uses the system photo picker, which needs no permission). Notifications: job, renewal and daily reminders. Texts go from the user's own Messages app; Tradie never sends them itself.

REGIONS
The app follows the device region (UK or US) and can be switched in Account → Your business: currency, postcode/ZIP, job names, and UK (HMRC) or US (federal) tax estimates. New in 1.7: UK VAT-registered users can set a VAT rate per item and mark a price as domestic reverse charge (CIS); US users can set a sales tax rate in Account → Prices and tax. Tax figures are planning estimates, not tax advice.

DATA
No adverts and no tracking. Anonymous analytics (Aptabase) records only steps such as "invoice sent", never names, amounts or anything typed, and isn't linked to the user. Only postcodes/ZIP codes are sent to Apple Maps for drive times. The app never asks for the microphone; dictation uses the iPhone keyboard.
