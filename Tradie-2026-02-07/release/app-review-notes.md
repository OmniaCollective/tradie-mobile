Tradie is a job, quote, invoice and tax set-aside app for self-employed tradespeople in the UK and US.

NO DEMO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Sign in with Apple is optional and is not asked for at the start. All jobs, customers, quotes, invoices and expenses are stored on the device only. Signing in (Account → Sign in, with your own Apple ID) only adds keeping Pro on a new phone and real drive times in Suggest times.

QUICK TEST PATH
1. Welcome → Get started → choose a trade → enter any details → Start using Tradie (or Skip for now).
2. Home → New job → enter a customer and a job type → Save.
3. The job opens as a checklist: Quote, Booked, Job done, Invoice, Paid. Steps can be done in any order and undone.
4. Send quote (PDF via the share sheet). Booked: Suggest times picks three free slots and drafts a text to the customer, or Pick a time myself.
5. Mark done → Send invoice → Mark paid.
6. Money tab: what you're owed, what's been paid, and (with Pro) the tax set-aside, expenses and the tax-year export. Tap an invoice for Send, Chase payment, Mark paid and Delete. Sending or chasing asks for business and payment details once if they are missing.
7. Account → Insurance and licences: add one to see expiry reminders.

SUBSCRIPTION (Tradie Pro, auto-renewable, monthly or yearly)
Free: unlimited jobs, quotes, customers and booking, and 3 invoices a month (counted when sent). Pro adds unlimited invoices, the tax set-aside (and VAT tracker in the UK), expenses with receipt photos, and the tax-year export for an accountant.
Open the paywall from Account → Plan → See Pro prices, or tap Tax set-aside or Expenses on the Money tab. The paywall shows the price, renewal terms, Restore purchase, Terms of use and Privacy policy links. Sandbox purchases work as normal.
Tax-year export (Pro): Money → Export the tax year for your accountant → This or Last tax year. It shares one zip file holding a summary PDF, a spreadsheet of income and expenses, and the receipt photos.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Account → Sign in → Delete account (shown when signed in). This revokes Sign in with Apple, deletes the server-side subscription record and erases all data on the device. The app tells the user to cancel any subscription in Apple ID settings. Account → Delete all my data erases the on-device data without an account.

PERMISSIONS (each is asked only when the feature is used)
Calendar: add booked jobs and check busy times for Suggest times. Camera and photos: receipts and job photos. Notifications: job, renewal and daily reminders. Messages are sent from the user's own Messages app; Tradie never sends texts itself.

REGIONS
The app follows the device region (UK or US) and can be switched in Account → Your business: currency, postcode/ZIP, job names, and UK (HMRC) or US (federal) tax estimates. Tax figures are estimates for planning, not tax advice, and are labelled as such.

DATA
No adverts and no tracking. Anonymous usage analytics (Aptabase) records only journey steps such as "invoice sent", never names, amounts or anything typed, and is not linked to the user. Only postcodes/ZIP codes are sent to Apple Maps for drive times. The app never asks for the microphone; dictation uses the iPhone keyboard.
