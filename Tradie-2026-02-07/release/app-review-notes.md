Tradie is a job, quote, invoice and tax set-aside app for self-employed tradespeople in the UK and US.

NO DEMO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Sign in with Apple is optional. Tap "Not now" on the welcome screen to use the app without an account. All jobs, customers, quotes, invoices and expenses are stored on the device only. Signing in (with your own Apple ID) only adds: keeping Pro on a new phone, and real drive times in Suggest times.

QUICK TEST PATH
1. Welcome → Not now → choose a trade → enter any details → Let's go.
2. Home → Add a job → enter a customer and choose a job type → Save.
3. On the job: "Send quote as PDF" (share sheet), then "Customer approved the quote".
4. "Suggest times" picks three free slots and drafts a text to the customer, or use "Pick a time myself" to book.
5. Start job → Mark job done → Create invoice.
6. Money tab: tap the invoice for Send, Mark paid, Chase payment and Delete. Sending or chasing asks for business and payment details once if they are missing.
7. Account → Insurance and licences: add one to see expiry reminders.

SUBSCRIPTION (Tradie Pro, auto-renewable, monthly or yearly)
Free: unlimited jobs, quotes and customers, and 3 invoices per month. Pro adds unlimited invoices, the tax and VAT tracker, and expenses with exports.
Open the paywall from Account → Free plan → Upgrade, or Money → "Unlock with Pro". The paywall shows the price, renewal terms, Restore purchase, Terms of use and Privacy policy links. Sandbox purchases work as normal.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Account → Delete account (shown when signed in). This revokes Sign in with Apple, deletes the server-side subscription record and erases all data on the device. The app tells the user to cancel any subscription in Apple ID settings.

PERMISSIONS (each is asked only when the feature is used)
Calendar: add booked jobs and check busy times for Suggest times. Camera and photos: receipts and job photos. Notifications: job, renewal and daily reminders. Messages are sent from the user's own Messages app; Tradie never sends texts itself.

REGIONS
The app follows the device region (UK or US) and can be switched in Account → Country: currency, postcode/ZIP, job names, and UK (HMRC) or US (federal) tax estimates. Tax figures are estimates for planning, not tax advice, and are labelled as such.

DATA
No adverts, no tracking. Only postcodes/ZIP codes are sent to Apple Maps for drive times. The app never asks for the microphone; dictation uses the iPhone keyboard.
