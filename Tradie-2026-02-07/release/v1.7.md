# Tradie v1.7 — release notes (draft)

Status: build 1.7 (32) uploaded to App Store Connect 15 Oct 2026. Not yet submitted for review.

## Offer

- **Free:** unlimited jobs, customers and itemised quotes; 3 invoices a month; logo, notes
  on quotes and invoices; the job Profit box (parts only).
- **Pro:** adds price list and parts markup to unlimited invoices, tax set-aside + VAT
  tracker, expenses and receipts, exports, voice jobs.
- "Powered by Tradie" stays on every PDF, Pro included.
- A free invoice now counts the first time its PDF leaves the app (send, send again,
  share or chase), not when it's marked as sent.

## What's New (App Store text)

Itemised quotes and invoices
• Add as many items as you need, each with its own quantity and price: labour, materials, a call-out fee or a discount.
• Your logo at the top of every quote and invoice.
• Add your own note or terms, and choose how long quotes are valid.
• Pro: save your regular prices in a price list, and add the parts you've logged to a price with your markup.

Tax
• VAT-registered? Set the VAT rate for each item, and use the reverse charge for CIS work.
• CIS deductions now come off labour only, as HMRC expects.
• US: add your sales tax rate, on materials only or on everything.

Easier to use
• Forms open as full-height cards, with Cancel and Save at the top, clear of the keyboard.
• A Done button above the keyboard on every screen.
• The job screen shows what's on the invoice, and profit uses what you actually charged.
• PDFs print on A4 in the UK, fit on one page where they can, and break cleanly when they can't.

Your existing quotes and invoices come across with every total unchanged.

## Data upgrade (store version 10)

- Every quote and invoice is turned into lines (Labour, Materials, Travel, Emergency
  call-out). Saved VAT and totals are kept exactly; nothing is recalculated.
- Invoices not yet sent with CIS have their deduction corrected to labour only. Sent and
  paid invoices keep theirs.
