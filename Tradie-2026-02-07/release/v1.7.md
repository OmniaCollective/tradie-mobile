# Tradie v1.7 — release notes (draft)

Status: merged into `main`. Not yet built on EAS or submitted.

## Offer

- **Free:** unlimited jobs, customers and itemised quotes; 3 invoices a month; logo, notes
  on quotes and invoices; the job Profit box (parts only).
- **Pro:** adds price list and parts markup to unlimited invoices, tax set-aside + VAT
  tracker, expenses and receipts, exports, voice jobs.
- "Powered by Tradie" stays on every PDF, Pro included.
- A free invoice now counts the first time its PDF leaves the app (send, send again,
  share or chase), not when it's marked as sent.

## What's New (App Store text)

Itemised quotes and invoices
- Add as many items as you need, each with a quantity and price. Labour, materials, a
  call-out fee or a discount.
- Your logo on every quote and invoice.
- Add your own note or terms to quotes and invoices, and choose how long quotes are valid.
- Pro: save your prices in a price list, and bring the parts you've logged into a price
  with your markup.
- VAT-registered? Set a VAT rate per item, and use the reverse charge for CIS work.
- US: add your sales tax rate.
- PDFs now print on A4 in the UK and run cleanly onto more than one page.
- CIS deductions now come off labour only, as HMRC expects.

## Data upgrade (store version 10)

- Every quote and invoice is turned into lines (Labour, Materials, Travel, Emergency
  call-out). Saved VAT and totals are kept exactly; nothing is recalculated.
- Invoices not yet sent with CIS have their deduction corrected to labour only. Sent and
  paid invoices keep theirs.
