Brand system v4

Neutrals do the work. The logo cyan (#00F5F5), the same in both modes, means “do this” or “money in”; text on it is navy. In light mode, cyan text would vanish on white, so links use the logo’s deep teal. One red means “needs attention now”. Nothing else carries colour. Every text colour passes WCAG AA in both modes.
